How It Works

You only pay after you've seen the finished system, confirmed it works as expected, and are happy with the result.

  1. Tell me what you need.
    Send me the requirements and any references.
  2. I build it.
    I develop the system based on your requirements.
  3. Review the result.
    I send you a walkthrough of the completed system.
  4. Request changes.
    You test it and give me feedback.
  5. Approve and pay.
    Once you're satisfied, you pay for the work.
  6. Delivery.
    I send the .rbxl or integrate the system into your game.
  7. Final testing.
    You test everything in-game and I handle any final fixes.

FAQ

Anything else, send me a message on Discord.

What do you write in?

Luau in Roblox Studio. The code is modular and the important checks live on the server.

How long does it take?

Small systems can take a few days. Larger systems may take a week or longer. I give you an estimate after reviewing the requirements.

What do you need to start?

A short description of what you want and any references. I'll reply with timing, a price and, if there is one, a simpler way to build it.

Do you work with existing games?

Yes. I can build new systems or work with existing code when the project is suitable.

Can you work from a reference?

Yes. Screenshots, videos and examples are helpful when explaining how you want something to work.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. If you have your own template, send it over.

Do you work for revenue share?

Usually I work on fixed payment. I'll look at revenue share case by case.

What don't you take on?

I currently don't take on anticheat, vehicle systems, or mechanics involving complex physics or geometry. FPS shooters are fine.
